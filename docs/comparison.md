# Why choose OvertChat?

**Make your local models an everyday app for you and your family.**
OvertChat brings a focused chat interface, speech, search, and family access
together, with your conversations on a server you control.

- **Bundled speech and search. Zero API keys.** OvertChat ships with
  speech-to-text, text-to-speech, and SearXNG web search. Select the bundled
  services in the [Linux installer](deploy.md); it installs and connects them
  for you. Bring your running local model and chat, dictate, listen, and search
  without provider accounts or API keys. No separate SearXNG setup.
- **Search has a backup.** If the primary search provider fails, enabled search
  automatically tries fallbacks, including free providers that need no API key.
  [How search fallbacks work](../README.md#search-with-a-backup-plan).
- **Keep the conversation easy to follow.** Expand reasoning and tool activity
  when useful, organize chats and files into projects, or talk back and forth
  in the browser. Interrupt spoken answers and return to the saved transcript.
  [See the interface](../README.md#the-useful-parts-together).
- **Set it up once for the household.** Connect your models, add family
  accounts, and share your server URL. Each person gets separate conversations
  and projects in the browser and native Android app. You choose the providers,
  keep the history, and control access.

## Compared with Open WebUI

Open WebUI suits people who want an extensive AI workspace: shared channels,
knowledge bases, model evaluations, and custom extensions.
[Explore its features](https://docs.openwebui.com/features/).

**Choose OvertChat for local chat with speech and search supplied together.** Open
WebUI's basic install is straightforward and includes local dictation; its
documented SearXNG integration involves deploying a search service and
configuring the connection. OvertChat handles that through the same wizard
as local speech and voice.
[Open WebUI quick start](https://docs.openwebui.com/getting-started/quick-start/),
[SearXNG setup](https://docs.openwebui.com/features/chat-conversations/web-search/providers/searxng/).

## Compared with LibreChat

LibreChat suits people who want to build custom assistants, connect tools,
execute code, and generate interactive content. It supports multiple users
and organizational access controls.
[Explore its features](https://www.librechat.ai/docs/features).

**Choose OvertChat for a complete local chat setup for the household.**
LibreChat supports browser speech, but its self-hosted speech and
SearXNG guides require separately running and connecting those services.
OvertChat bundles and configures those services for you. Coding agents remain
optional.
[LibreChat speech setup](https://www.librechat.ai/docs/configuration/stt_tts),
[search setup](https://www.librechat.ai/docs/configuration/librechat_yaml/object_structure/web_search).

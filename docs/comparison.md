# Why choose OvertChat?

**A polished home for local AI that you can run for yourself and your family.**
OvertChat focuses on the everyday chat experience: a clear interface, useful
built-ins, and easy setup. You own the chat surface, the server, and the database.

- **Your models, available to everyone at home.** Connect vLLM, llama.cpp, or
  SGLang once, then create accounts for your family. Everyone gets their own
  conversations and projects through the browser or native Android app.
- **Speech and search come with the stack.** The guided installer offers
  Parakeet speech-to-text, Kokoro text-to-speech, and SearXNG search. With your
  local model, you can chat, dictate, hear answers, and search without signing
  up for an API provider or buying credits.
- **Voice is part of the conversation.** Talk back and forth in the browser,
  interrupt an answer, use search, and return to the saved transcript.
- **Your data stays under your control.** Chat history lives in SQLite on your
  server. You choose the models and services, manage accounts, and own backups.
- **MIT licensed.** Modify, redistribute, and rebrand OvertChat while keeping
  the [copyright and license notice](../LICENSE). No added branding conditions.

Coding agents are available when you need them. Everyday local AI is the focus.

## Compared with Open WebUI

Choose OvertChat for its focused UI and guided installation of the local chat,
search, and speech stack. Open WebUI offers a wider feature and configuration
surface, and also supports local models and keyless operation. Its license
includes a branding restriction with exceptions; OvertChat uses MIT.
[Open WebUI setup](https://docs.openwebui.com/getting-started/quick-start/),
[license terms](https://github.com/open-webui/open-webui/blob/0a7c15832fb30b1903753e83f81dc7d27e5b0944/LICENSE).

## Compared with LibreChat

Choose OvertChat for local AI with bundled services managed through one setup
flow. LibreChat emphasizes a broad provider and agent ecosystem. Its default
stack uses MongoDB, Meilisearch, pgvector, and a RAG API; OvertChat keeps chat
history and history search in SQLite. LibreChat is also MIT licensed.
[LibreChat deployment](https://github.com/danny-avila/LibreChat/blob/0354db3b57445324d476ec054140c612e77cac04/docker-compose.yml),
[features and license](https://github.com/danny-avila/LibreChat/blob/0354db3b57445324d476ec054140c612e77cac04/README.md).
